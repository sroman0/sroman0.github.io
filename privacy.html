Privacy notice

Last revised: 6 October 2026

This notice describes the personal portfolio operated by Simone Romano, based in Italy. For privacy questions, email simoneromano221@gmail.com.

Visiting this site

This portfolio has no accounts, payment checkout, or contact forms. Its page scripts do not include advertising or analytics trackers. Fonts and page assets are served with the site.

Cloudflare hosts the portfolio and processes network information to deliver and protect it. This can include your IP address and information about requests and traffic. Hosting services may keep operational and security logs. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy for its data practices.

Appearance preference

If you choose a light or dark appearance, the site saves that choice in your browser’s local storage under the key theme. The page scripts do not send this preference to a server. You can remove it by clearing this site’s browser storage. Without a saved choice, the site follows your device preference.

Email enquiries

If you email me, I receive your email address, message, and any attachments. I use that information to respond and handle the enquiry. Correspondence is kept in my email account while it is needed for that conversation and relevant follow-up.

You can email me to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent. Some records may need to be kept where required by law.

External projects and profiles

GitHub, LinkedIn, project demos, and other linked websites have their own privacy practices. Following a link takes you to that service. The Shattered Bytes demo is hosted separately on GitHub Pages.
